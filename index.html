About the data

All data comes from the public stats on opentibia.info (Project Fibula): the online list, character pages and the site's Players scanner. Nothing is read from the game itself.

  • Online status is collected every 5 minutes by a scheduled GitHub job. Together with GitHub's file cache, a login or logout normally shows here within 5 to 10 minutes.
  • This page updates itself every minute, so it can stay open on a second screen. Refresh now checks at once.
  • Alt scan runs every night at 04:17 (Norwegian time). It refreshes level, vocation and guild and adds new members of the tracked guilds. Nobody is ever removed.
  • Own alt scanner: every login and logout on the server is logged from the online list. Each night it looks for characters that log in right when a tracked character logs out (and the other way round) on several days, then checks the exact times on their character pages. Hits are added under the owner and marked new. It gets better the more days of data it has (it keeps 21 days).
  • Alt links are based on matching login and logout times over the last 90 days. They are strong hints, not proof.
Instant view straight from opentibia.info
  1. Create a new bookmark in your browser and paste the script as its address (URL).
  2. Open opentibia.info with Fibula selected.
  3. Click the bookmark. A panel lists the tracked characters online right now, with level, vocation and owner, and refreshes every 60 seconds.
